1. Summary

Odium sells a desktop license. To deliver and protect that license we need a small amount of identifying data — a Discord account, a hardware fingerprint, and a record of your order. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising trackers, and the software does not log your gameplay or your keystrokes.

2. What we collect

  • Discord identity — user ID, username and avatar, collected when you authenticate through Discord OAuth.
  • Hardware identifier — a one-way hash derived from your machine's hardware, used to bind the license. The raw values behind the hash are not stored.
  • Order data — order reference, product, amount, currency, payment method and timestamp.
  • Payment identifiers — a transaction ID or invoice ID supplied by the payment processor. Card numbers never reach our servers.
  • Support messages — the contents of tickets you open, kept so a case can be followed up.

3. What we do not collect

  • No gameplay telemetry, screenshots, keylogs or process lists from your machine.
  • No browsing history from this site beyond standard server request logs.
  • No card numbers, CVVs or full bank details — those stay with the payment processor.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking pixels.

4. How data is used

Discord identity and order data are used to deliver the key, attach it to the correct account, and verify ownership during support. The hardware hash is used to keep one license on one machine and to process reset requests. Support messages are used to resolve the issue that was raised.

We do not use your data for profiling, automated decision making, or marketing to third parties.

5. Payments

Cryptocurrency payments are processed through a hosted invoice provider. PayPal payments are processed by PayPal under its own privacy policy. Cash App payments are processed by Block, Inc. Card payments are processed by Stripe, Inc., and card data is entered on Stripe's hosted checkout page, never on this site.

We receive only the confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount, and an identifier that lets the payment be matched to an order reference.

6. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no advertising cookies. The checkout page stores a single order reference in your browser's session storage so the reference survives a page reload; it is cleared when the tab is closed. Anything else the site needs to remember is stored server side against your account.

7. Sharing

Data is shared only with the processors needed to run the service: the Discord API for authentication, the payment processor for the method you chose, and the hosting provider for the storefront and database. Data is not sold, rented or traded.

Data may be disclosed where required by a valid legal order directed at the operator, limited to what that order compels.

8. Retention

Order records are retained for as long as the license is active, plus the period needed for accounting and dispute resolution. Support tickets are retained for twelve months after the last message. Hardware hashes are deleted when a license is removed.

9. Security

Traffic to this site is encrypted in transit. Credentials for third-party services are stored server side and never exposed to the browser. Access to order and license records is limited to staff who need it to fulfil an order or resolve a ticket. No system is perfectly secure, and you should assume that any account identifier you hand to any online service could eventually be exposed.

10. Your rights

You can request a copy of the data tied to your account, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion of data that is not required for an active license or for accounting. Requests are handled through the support ticket system and answered within 30 days.

11. Age

The service is restricted to users aged 18 and over. Data from anyone under that age is removed on discovery, together with the associated license.

12. Changes

This policy is updated when the data the service handles changes. The revision date at the top of the page always reflects the current version.

13. Contact
